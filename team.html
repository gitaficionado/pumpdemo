About · Team

The PUMP-CS Team

University faculty and Wisconsin classroom teachers who plan and lead PUMP-CS professional learning.

Project director

Dr. Dennis Brylow

Professor and Chair of Computer Science, Marquette University

Dr. Brylow has led PUMP-CS since 2010. He co-chaired the committee that wrote Wisconsin's first K–12 computer science standards, directs Marquette's partnership with Code.org as its Regional Partner for Wisconsin, and leads the PISCES Lab, which has earned four NSF awards for CS teacher preparation and research since 2014.

Read Dr. Brylow's profile

Facilitators

Led by Wisconsin teachers

Members of the Wisconsin-Dairyland chapter of the Computer Science Teachers Association form most of the facilitator corps that plans and runs PUMP-CS professional development. Facilitators are classroom teachers who have taught the courses they lead.

The chapter launched at an early PUMP-CS gathering at Marquette, and it continues to advise on every PD offering.

Advisors

  • CSTA Wisconsin-Dairyland advises on PD offerings and supplies most facilitators.
  • CSforWI ECEP Alliance team advises on PD offerings.
