Partners & supporters

Partners & Supporters

PUMP-CS plans its professional development with Wisconsin's CS teaching community and has been funded by federal, nonprofit, and industry partners.

Advisors & facilitators

Planned with Wisconsin's CS teachers

The Wisconsin-Dairyland chapter of CSTA and the CSforWI ECEP Alliance team advise PUMP-CS on its PD offerings. WI CSTA members form most of the facilitator corps that plans and runs PUMP-CS professional development.

CSTA Wisconsin-Dairyland

Launched at an early PD gathering hosted at Marquette. Advises on PD offerings and supplies most of the facilitator corps.

CSforWI ECEP Alliance

Wisconsin's team in the national Expanding Computing Education Pathways Alliance. Advises on PD offerings.

Partner institutions

Schools, universities, and researchers

Milwaukee Public Schools

Long-term research and practice partner for ECS and districtwide CS access.

Wisconsin DPI

A founding collaborator in 2010 and the agency behind Wisconsin's K–12 CS standards.

UW–La Crosse

Co-led the 2014 NSF two-track teacher preparation sequence.

UW–Milwaukee

Collaborator on the 2017–2020 NSF award to certify MPS teachers.

Sacred Heart University (CT)

Partner on Project {FUTURE}, with Cooperative Educational Services in Connecticut.

American Institutes for Research

Research and evaluation partner on the 2019 and 2025 NSF awards.

The Learning Partnership

Evaluation partner on the 2019 and 2025 NSF awards.

Regional partners

CESA 7, Milwaukee Tech Hub Coalition, NEW IT Alliance, and colleges and districts that host regional cohorts.
