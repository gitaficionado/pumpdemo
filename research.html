Research

Research on CS Teaching in Wisconsin

PUMP-CS studies how computer science is taught and how its teachers are prepared, in partnership with Milwaukee Public Schools and national research organizations.

Research that shapes practice

Alongside professional development, PUMP-CS conducts research on computer science teaching and teacher preparation. The goal is practical: learn what teachers need to implement CS courses well, and feed that back into the next round of professional learning.

In 2019, a nearly $2 million NSF research-practice partnership with Milwaukee Public Schools, The Learning Partnership, and the American Institutes for Research began studying how early CS exposure shapes later learning, why CS initiatives work unevenly across a city, and how to strengthen CS education in an urban district.

The 2025 NSF Computer Science for All award continues this work, measuring results with the Capacity, Access, Participation, Experience (CAPE) framework that MPS uses to promote equitable CS education.

Research questions

  • How does early exposure to CS affect students' later education?
  • Why do CS initiatives work unevenly across schools in one district?
  • What support do teachers need to implement ECS and other courses?
  • How many qualified CS teachers does Wisconsin have, and where?
