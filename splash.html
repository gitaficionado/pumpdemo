Preparing the Upper Midwest for Principles of Computer Science (PUMP-CS) is a collaborative community of educators, researchers, and tech advocates dedicated to ensuring equitable access to high-quality computing education for every student in Wisconsin.

Our Mission

Leading action to systemic impact

By empowering in-service teachers with content mastery and inclusive pedagogical practices, we bridge equity gaps and build sustainable computing pathways across urban and rural school systems.

Equitable Classrooms

Broadening Participation

We work closely with partners like Milwaukee Public Schools to deliver culturally responsive computer science instruction, helping underserved, Black, and Hispanic student populations see themselves as the next generation of tech innovators.
